# Leaving Certificate Mathematics Coverage Audit

Checked 9 October 2026 against the [current NCCA syllabus](https://www.curriculumonline.ie/getmedia/f6f2e822-2b0c-461e-bcd4-dfcde6decc0c/SCSEC25_Maths_syllabus_examination-2015_English.pdf). The [subject page](https://www.curriculumonline.ie/senior-cycle/senior-cycle-subjects/mathematics/) lists this examinable syllabus alongside a specification under consultation. The board does not substitute draft material for the current course.

The 83 rows are a tutor-facing grouping, not an official count of NCCA topics. Each row stores its source section. Ordinary Level outcomes form the shared core; Higher Level adds the explicitly labelled material. Foundation Level requires its own checklist and is not claimed here. Corresponding Junior Cycle knowledge is assumed; arithmetic and graph-reading prerequisites are recapped where useful. Modelling, synthesis, reasoning, communicating methods and checking results apply across all five strands.

## Level Boundaries

- Permutations are shared; combinations and binomial coefficients are HL.
- Expected value is shared. General conditional rules, binomial counts, kth-success problems and normal-table calculations are HL.
- The approximate proportion margin of error and its hypothesis-test use are shared. z-based confidence intervals, mean tests and p-values are HL.
- Complex arithmetic, modulus, conjugate and Argand plotting are shared. Polar form, conjugate roots, De Moivre and nth roots are HL.
- Recognising geometric growth is shared; geometric-series calculations, limits, induction and present-value applications are HL.
- Index laws, compound interest, depreciation, tax, margin and the trapezoidal rule are shared at Leaving Cert, unlike some Junior Cycle boundaries.
- OL includes line/circle intersections for origin-centred circles; general-circle intersection problems and further coordinate techniques are HL.
- Constructions 16-21 are shared; the orthocentre construction is HL. Prescribed formal proofs 11-13 are examinable at HL, with earlier geometry assumed.
- Sine/cosine rules and degree-based sectors are shared. Radians, 3D trigonometry, trigonometric graphs/equations and appendix identity work are HL.
- Composition and linear/quadratic/cubic differentiation are shared. Inverses, completing the square, first principles, extended derivative rules and integration are HL.

Shared rows with an HL extension explain that extension separately in the popup. Ordinary profiles omit wholly HL-only rows and suppress those extension labels; Higher profiles show the full board. Separate `lc-` IDs prevent old Junior Cycle colours being silently reassigned to new concepts.
